# Glyph brief — for Comfy, not a bible amendment

Source: `loom/DESIGN-BIBLE.md` version one, 2026-09-04.
Artwork production is out of scope for the bible. This file is the
brief that gets handed to the generator. Do not copy Valve art.
Take the grammar, not the costume. Orange is out.

Window of the weave is a white sheet (owner, 2026-09-05). A tile
is a mark on that sheet. Default field is white. `--field sign`
is the charter genre: white marks on a dark sign face.

## Grammar

- One glyph. Fixed square tile. Even padding. Nothing else.
- Two frames: outer shape is borrowed (BPMN / flowchart), never
  invented. Squared = settled. Cloud = still forming. Inner mark
  is the thing, and it is a swappable skin.
- Round silhouette = human. Boxy = machine.
- Two classes: nouns and imperatives. Nouns split three ways:
  personas, processes, tools. A hat is a role on a persona. The
  body does not change.
- State is fill, not a second glyph. Hollow = not started. Solid
  = done. Do not read progress from how full the fill is.
- Null tile: something should be here and is not. Not the same as
  empty black (nothing exists).
- Hazard is a modifier on a noun, colour `#D55E00`. Task `#56B4E9`
  means look here. Changed `#CC79A7` is italics / moved-since.
  At most two accents. Precedence: task, hazard, changed. Orange
  is forbidden.
- No numbers on the tile. Sequence lives outside the grid.
- No caption, no word, no logo, no UI chrome, no decoration.

## How to sit

Describe the thing. Queue. If it is wrong, queue again (new seed).
When one is right, pull it. Do not edit the lock nodes.
